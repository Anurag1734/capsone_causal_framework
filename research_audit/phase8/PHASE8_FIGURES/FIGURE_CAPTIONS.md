# Figure captions

1. **Cohort flow.** Admission-level selection from the adult denominator to ECG-selected admissions and the acute, history-only, and clean-comparison groups. The selection box also reports the denominator admissions without an eligible ECG. The primary Layer 2 comparison excludes the history-only group.
2. **Proposed causal graph.** Roadmap-based hypothesized two-layer structure and ECG selection mechanism. The diagnosis-to-feature arrow is an assumption, not identified temporal direction. The prior-MI edge is descriptive.
3. **Layer 1 estimates.** AIPW adjusted risk differences for coded prior diabetes, hypertension, CKD, and MI predicting acute MI diagnosis in the ECG-selected adult-admission cohort. Horizontal bars show patient-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals.
4. **Layer 2 estimates.** AIPW adjusted mean differences for all 95 features, standardized by each feature's primary-cohort SD. Error bars are transformed Phase 5 intervals. The technical support measure is distinct.
5. **Crude versus adjusted.** Crude and adjusted mean differences for the same 95 features, divided by each outcome SD. The diagonal denotes equal estimates.
6. **Feature groups.** Mean absolute standardized adjusted estimates among physiological groups; labels give BH-significant feature counts. Correlated features are not independent replications.
7. **FDR results.** Estimates and intervals for features passing the primary 95-feature BH FDR 5% family. One technical support feature is shown separately.
8. **Sensitivity comparison.** Standardized primary effects plotted against selection-weighted and broader-current-code estimates for all 95 features. The broad definition changes the case mix and attenuates mean ST/T contrasts.
9. **Age and sex effect modification.** Direct interaction contrasts among features passing BH q<0.05, standardized by the primary-cohort feature SD. Within-subgroup significance alone is not used to infer effect modification.
10. **Model-implied profiles.** Phase 6 predictions with the acute diagnosis indicator set to 0 or 1 at three fixed covariate profiles. These are exploratory outcome-model outputs, not observed counterfactual ECGs; no profile-specific intervals were estimated.
