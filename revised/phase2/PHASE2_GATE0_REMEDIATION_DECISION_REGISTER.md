## Decision summary

Codex recommends ≥2 readings as the measurement-count candidate and retains 365 days only as a proposed horizon. Data support streamability and a common-index aggregate match, but they do **not** support complete fixed-horizon outcome observation, complete death ascertainment, or the prespecified disease adjustment/subgroup profiles. The protocol/intervention remain unchanged. The team must decide whether the resulting limitations are acceptable and what additional sources or approved design clarifications are required. The package does not authorize cohort construction or causal estimation.
