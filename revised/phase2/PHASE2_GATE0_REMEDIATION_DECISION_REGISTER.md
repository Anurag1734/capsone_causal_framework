## Decision summary

Codex recommends ≥2 readings as the measurement-count candidate and retains 365 days only as a proposed horizon. Data support streamability and a common-index aggregate match, but they do **not** support complete fixed-horizon outcome observation, complete death ascertainment, or the prespecified disease adjustment/subgroup profiles. Prepared-source inspection confirms the disease-history and observation gaps; it does not resolve them. The original protocol/intervention remain unchanged. Review options A/B/C in `PHASE2_IDENTIFIABILITY_AND_ESTIMAND_DECISION.md`; the team must select the path and approve any changed interpretation. Gate 0 remains failed; no cohort construction or causal estimation is authorized.
