# Phase 2.0 Decision Register

> **Status update:** Rows below document the initial Phase 2.0 decisions and blockers. For current empirical evidence on BP thresholds, follow-up, death, positivity, and subgroup precision, use `PHASE2_GATE0_REMEDIATION_DECISION_REGISTER.md` and `PHASE2_GATE0_REMEDIATION_REPORT.md`. The remediation package does not close all blockers and does not authorize cohort construction.

Status categories follow the task definition. “Recommended; empirical validation required” is not human approval. Primary cohort construction remains gated by `PHASE2_GATE_CRITERIA.md`.
